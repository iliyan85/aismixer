Provenance for the single published Type 1 telemetry fixture in
ais_codec_tests.cpp and ais_wire_oracle.py:

Eric S. Raymond / GPSD project, AIVDM/AIVDO protocol decoding,
version 1.58 (24 June 2023), AIVDM/AIVDO Sentence Layer.
https://gpsd.gitlab.io/gpsd/AIVDM.html#_aivdm_aivdo_sentence_layer
https://gitlab.com/gpsd/gpsd/-/raw/master/www/AIVDM.adoc

Only the short published NMEA data sentence is reused. No GPSD decoder or
other implementation code is copied. The synthetic goldens and both decoder
oracles in this laboratory are independently implemented.
