## Entities, clocks and local authority

Requirements: A0-GEN-001/002/007/008/009, A0-SIM-001/005/007/008, A0-INT-001/002/005/007/011/012, A0-MAC-001..010, A0-RF-001..005/013..015/024.

A vessel is physical W1 truth; its movement continues with AIS OFF. A ship's Class A equipment couples TX/RX power but has its own protocol power epoch, clock, configuration, message queues, directory and channel maps. A passive terrestrial receiver has independent power and RF/playback duties, without a Class A slot-selection FSM. Electrical ON begins observation, not transmit readiness. Protocol state resets on a new power epoch under project policy; own identity and persistent regional channel configuration retain their separately specified lifetime.

The standard's topology is two simultaneous terrestrial receive processes and **one transmitter**, alternating channels/frequencies. Default terrestrial AIS1 and AIS2 are161.975 and162.025MHz. The complete Class A transmit inventory additionally includes satellite75/76 at156.775/156.825MHz for Message27. These are not four independently transmitting radios. Shared-transmitter turnaround, no TX while switching, same-channel follow-ups and opposite-channel own-slot exclusions constrain local scheduling. An AIS1/AIS2-only initial pilot explicitly limits its coverage; it cannot claim all Class A duties have disappeared. Channel access and received reservations are channel-specific even though own transmitter coordination spans the two maps.

Each station accepts only timestamped **own** navigation/EPFS input, explicit **own** clock/configuration/power, its own timers and dispatched ownership-validated **own-radio** tokens. It cannot read remote W1 positions, another station's map, the global RF journal, future transmissions, propagation winners or a perfect-hearing directory. Range used for reuse comes from locally decoded position and own valid position, never a remote-world query. A passive receiver's decode is not automatically a ship decode. A directory entry records last-heard time/channel, valid position/source, synchronization evidence and communication-state allocation provenance, all at disclosure time.

The direct-UTC pilot assumption is explicit per independent station: valid source, UTC mapping, source/phase/error/quality and validity history. Runtime exact time is not that source. Source preference is own UTC, eligible received UTC-direct source, qualified base, eligible mobile directly synchronized to a base, qualified mobile semaphore. UTC indirect and base indirect permit only the specified single indirect level. Base/mobile selection uses local freshness/count evidence and lower-MMSI tie-break; base needs two reports in40s; mobile counts span nine frames. Tables9-11 define sync-state and semaphore qualifications, excluding the station being followed from the relevant highest-received-state calculation. A mobile semaphore changes to2s reporting and retains that support until qualification has been invalid3min. Receive processing is not gated to slot boundaries. Phase synchronization needs the complete received end flag and valid FCS; missing source or invalid evidence never becomes invented UTC-direct lock. A0-RF-031 records mobile source-relative timing error within104 microseconds, accumulated312 microseconds and the nominal9600bit/s tolerance50ppm; base tolerances differ. A pilot can explicitly choose ideal nominal clocks, but that is a profile assumption, not validation of oscillator/holdover behavior.

### First emission and first complete minute

A2-3.3.5.1-.3/Figures12-14 (printed38-41) supply the transaction. After the observation minute, choose NSS_A between the current slot and NI forward; equal-rate dual-channel NSS_B is offset by NI. Set first NS=NSS; build candidates around NS from **local** maps, randomly select NTS and initial timeout3..7, and mark it internally allocated. On failure restart NSS selection. At NTS, use Message3 with ITDMA communication state. The label RATDMA describes the first unannounced access; it does not turn Message3 into a SOTDMA packet or invoke the generic probability gate. A0-ISS-003 leaves per-channel first-frame origin and terminal-zero timing open when the two initial transmissions are staggered, and at low rates; the named aggregate state does not choose one global cutoff implicitly.

Before each first-frame emission compute the next NS/NTS, reserve it locally and advertise its same-channel increment, slot count and keep. Repeat for the elapsed minute; final increment0 terminates first-frame allocations. Only then use continuous SOTDMA scheduled reporting. A retained slot/announced future slot is a local expectation, not an exclusive global grant and not proof that anyone received it. Empty-network entry is valid with the explicit own clock/profile inputs and no invented peer messages. Two blind stations can independently choose overlap. A0.1 settles only the named limited continuous-mode readings; staggered-channel/low-rate first-frame and excluded fractional cases remain explicitly gated rather than hidden in a nominal successful example.

ON for less than60s produces **zero TX**. Reactivation repeats initialization and does not reuse stale protocol reservations. At exactly ON+60s observation is complete, but no transmission is invented without a selected lawful NTS and applicable inputs. OFF at exact T cancels unsent work. During a burst it truncates actual interference at T and prevents complete-frame decode. Current event schemas cannot represent that by a normal TransmissionEnd; the additive abort/terminal-disposition contract and exact-end tie must be designed before I1 acceptance. Previously valid journaled decodes remain valid for later wall-time playback.

## Slot maps, access and communication state

Requirements: A0-MAC-008/012..024/027/032..041, A0-RF-016/022/023/025, A0-MSG-001/009, A0-SIM-002/003/007.

`free`, `internal`, `external` and `garbled` describe **local** knowledge (A2-3.1.6, printed23). Aging and distance can make allocations free: unused SOTDMA allocations over the preceding three frames and beyond120NM cases have source-specific treatment. Undecodable energy above16dB over background is garble, but Class A's slot-state distinction treats garbled as free; repeaters differ. Garble never yields decoded MMSI, position, synchronization or communication state.

Construct candidates from slots free on both channels first; account for own TX opposite/adjacent exclusions and protected reservations. A multi-slot candidate starts a complete consecutive usable block. Aim for at least four candidates, supplementing by permitted reuse, then select equiprobably regardless of how a candidate entered the set. Reuse requires own position, protects positionless peers and bases within120NM, prefers the most distant eligible locally known station and excludes that reused station from further intentional reuse for one frame. The ordered source Rule1..5 is: selected/other channel = free/reusable, reusable/free, reusable/reusable, free/nonreusable, reusable/nonreusable. Stop expansion at four or rule exhaustion. Here reusable means externally allocated but eligible under these protections, not merely occupied. If restrictions leave fewer, do not manufacture four. Use the current slot only if available; otherwise selection can fail. Figures9 and20 support excluding j-1,j,j+1 opposite an own TX at j, modulo2250; j-2/j+2 are not excluded by this rule alone (A0-MAC-040/LCP-P05). Independent base/mobile restrictions still apply.

Generic RATDMA is a separate priority/FIFO process (A2-3.3.4.2/Table14, printed31-33). Its selection interval is150 slots/four seconds. Initially candidate counter RTCSC is at least4, attempts RTA=0 and RTP2=RTPS=100/RTCSC. At a candidate, draw RTP1 in0..100, transmit for RTP1<=RTP2; otherwise update probability using RTPI=(100-RTP2)/RTCSC, decrement candidates and increment attempts as specified. A peer reservation can remove a candidate without consuming an attempt. Keep the original RTES deadline. A0.1 uses the reviewed prose comparator sum<4 with finite replenishment (A0-MAC-036/LCP-P03), and explicit rational arithmetic/inclusive integer draws (LCP-P04). Both official formats print the same negative sign in Table14 note2: the Word minus is a Symbol-font w:sym F02D that flattened text omitted. The positive maximum column/formula and priority1/0 bounds remain contradictory printed evidence (A0-MAC-041); no PDF-versus-Word sign disagreement exists. **Network entry is expressly exempt from this p-persistence procedure.** Neither generic p-persistence nor entry is equivalent to uniformly choosing one free slot once.

ITDMA semantic Table13 values are increment0..8191, block length1..5 and keep: true retains the present slot(s) one additional frame; false releases after transmission. Increment0 ends further allocation. The19-bit wire state is sync2 + increment13 + slot-count-code3 + keep1. Table20 codes0..4 mean lengths1..5; codes5..7 mean lengths1..3 with8192 added to the increment, supporting longer low-rate offsets. Those extended forms already occur in2014; they are not a newly invented A0 extension. Keep target absolute frame provenance and encode the wire-relative representation only at the boundary.

SOTDMA Table16 (printed35-36) defines Rr=60/RI, NI=2250/Rr, single-channel NS=NSS+n*NI and equal-rate dual-channel spacing2*NI with B shifted by NI. SI spans NS +/-0.1*NI. Figure11 coordinates B at its next NTS and does not assert synchronized nominal patterns during unequal reporting rates. Table16 prints maximum NI1225; the formula and minimum Rr2 derive1125, independently consistent with Table15's FTI limit (printed34) and Message16 code1 (printed125), while those are distinct protocol contexts. This is the bounded A0-MAC-035 derivation, not an official correction. The pilot's continuous autonomous NI is75,125,225 or375; LCP-P01 defines its integer SI and leaves assigned/fractional domains open. Normal randomized timeouts are3..7 inclusive; assigned-mode footnotes permit other report-rate/NI limits and must not be erased. Expiring continuous allocation advertises newNTS-currentNTS+2250, targeting the next frame, not an accidental same-frame modulo result.

SOTDMA19 = sync2 + timeout3 + submessage14 (Tables18-19, printed48). Timeout0 conveys next-frame offset (zero relinquishes);1 conveys UTC hour/minute **when UTC is available**, with two unused low bits;2/4/6 convey current slot;3/5/7 convey received-station count. Table19 does not explicitly supply the no-UTC timeout1 unavailable encoding; A0-ISS-004 gates that degraded-mode question rather than borrowing Message4 defaults. ITDMA and SOTDMA state apply to the carrying channel only. Message tables are MSB-first fields/subfields; the on-air process groups octets then emits each octet LSB-first, with stuffing and NRZI. Range-valid raw RadioState19/20 does not validate a semantic reservation.

## Exact time, RF envelope and causal frontier

Requirements: A0-TIM-001..004, A0-SIM-002..008, A0-RF-006..011/027..030, A0-INT-004..011, A0-MAC-007/009/034.

The integer derivation is U=9830400 units/s, F=60U=589824000 units/frame, L=F/2250=262144 units/slot, B=U/9600=1024 units/nominal bit. For a valid explicit UTC anchor a, normalized absolute unit count q=a.posix_seconds*U+a.subsecond_units+t.units gives minute=floor(q/F), remainder r=q-minute*F, slot=floor(r/L), in-slot=r-slot*L. Thus0<=slot<2250 and0<=in-slot<L, including negative anchors. Calculate with checked/wide intermediates; overflow rejects rather than wraps. Runtime POSIX time excludes leap seconds; leap-second discipline is not silently supplied by this mapping. A station's clock estimate/phase maps its own observed slot state separately from exact world time.

Physical intervals use the project's half-open convention [start,end), separately from nominal reserved slot spans. RF packet framing includes ramps,24-bit training, HDLC flags, data,16-bit FCS, data/FCS stuffing and decay/buffer, not NMEA fill or XOR. Default168-bit data gives modulation end at232+S bit-times from T0 (S actual stuffing), or237568+1024S units. Table6's one-stuff-bit TE233/TF241 threshold envelope differs from Fig8's zero-power T4=T3+1ms. One millisecond is9830.4 units: RF1 must resolve rational/quantized endpoints and the permitted power envelope before physical acceptance. A reserved256-bit slot is not continuous full-power RF. Multi-slot framing has one overhead, contiguous at most five slots and no filler; a two-slot Type5 packet is independent of receiver NMEA fragment count. ISO/IEC13239:2002 exact CRC/serialization oracle remains externally gated.

Only complete valid-frame/FCS evidence may supply decoded remote state. Truncation, a false frame or bad FCS gives no decoded allocation; competing energy alone gives none. An overlapping frame may update knowledge only if RF1 actually delivers a complete valid decode to that receiver. Carrier/garble evidence is separate. RF1 later evaluates propagation and per-receiver outcomes from valid datum-resolved geometry and full simulation-time TX/RX power intervals; W1 reach radius is not a decode criterion.

Advance a station only to a justified next local boundary: power, own navigation/clock change, initialization deadline, NTS/attempt, message deadline, reservation expiry, or newly disclosed own-radio evidence. Those boundaries may alter the next uncommitted request. RF batching is allowed behind the authority boundary; it cannot reveal a future result early or revise below the committed event watermark. No five-hour TDMA allocation can be precomputed without intervening feedback. Runtime Scheduler orders causal events and producer barriers; it never awards slots.

At the same instant existing phases permit local reception2 -> decision3 -> TX start4. Same-phase map changes cannot themselves be earlier causal parents of a decision at that instant. Lifecycle phase0 is effective at T; OFF versus physical-end phase1 needs a new formally reviewed contract. Candidate expiry/current-slot ties and fractional phase remain explicit review matters, not unstated ITU rules; LCP-P01/P04/P05 settle only their named integer windows/probability/adjacency domains. LCP-P08 versions the station's independent semantic Philox keys (seed, stable equipment identity, algorithm/version, purpose, epoch/episode, draw index), with persisted counters.
